# Marketing client layout

Client reference: marketing sketch supplied on 16 November 2026.

- Email, WhatsApp and Call tabs show activity counts and due follow-ups. All channels and Other channels retain access to earlier or custom channels. Existing Phone Call entries belong to Call.
- Each new activity selects a client from the client master list. A shortcut in a client record opens Marketing with that client preselected. Existing unlinked prospects remain editable.
- The record grid shows client, activity date, activity status, last contact date and details, next follow-up, response and channel. Column dragging, resizing and visibility controls remain available.
- Last contact is the latest actual recorded contact for that client across channels. Future plans do not replace contact history or reset client inactivity. Old entries use their original activity date and notes until edited.
- Contacted, Follow-up and Closed activities require a last-contact date and details. Future last-contact dates are rejected. Follow-ups due today or earlier are highlighted; closed activities do not count as due.
- New activities inherit the selected channel. Reports and CSV export follow the active channel and filters. Existing product and response reporting is retained.

This module records outreach and follow-ups. Selecting a channel or recording an activity does not automatically send a message or initiate a call.
